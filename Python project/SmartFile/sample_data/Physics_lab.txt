Experiment Report: Planck's Constant using Light Emitting Diodes
Lab Section: B-4 | Experiment Date: October 2026

1. Objective:
To determine the value of Planck's constant (h) by measuring the
threshold turn-on voltage (knee voltage) of different colored LEDs
of known peak emission wavelengths.

2. Formula and Principle:
The energy of emitted photon is related to electron charge and threshold voltage:
  E = h * nu = (h * c) / lambda = e * V0
Therefore:
  V0 = (h * c / e) * (1 / lambda)
Plotting V0 versus (1 / lambda) yields a straight line with slope:
  m = (h * c) / e

3. Observations:
LED Color    Wavelength (nm)    1/Wavelength (1/m)    Knee Voltage (V)
Red          650                1.538e6               1.85
Amber        590                1.695e6               2.02
Green        525                1.905e6               2.30
Blue         470                2.128e6               2.75

4. Conclusion:
The experimental value obtained for Planck's constant is h = 6.64e-34 J*s,
which is within 0.3% error of the standard literature value (6.626e-34 J*s).
